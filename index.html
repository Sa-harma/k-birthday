A letter for you

Some things are better written down, so you can keep them.

To my special person, ❤️

Today is your birthday, and we’re going to meet soon.

It feels so special knowing that while I’m writing this, it’s your birthday, and in just a little while, I’ll finally get to see you in person. After all the conversations, laughs, and moments we’ve shared, I honestly can’t wait for the moment when I finally get to stand in front of you.

I’ve thought about what I should give you for your birthday, but I wanted to give you something that could stay with you for a long time. Something you could keep and read again years from now.

So I wrote you this letter.

I hope that whenever you find it again, you remember this exact time in our lives—your birthday, our first meeting, and the excitement we had before creating so many more memories together.

I may be nervous when I see you. I might not know what to say. But one thing I know for sure is that I’ll be genuinely happy to finally meet the girl who has become so special to me. ❤️

I don’t know what the future has planned for us, but I know I want to enjoy every moment we get, make you smile, support you, annoy you a little 😄, and create memories that we’ll look back on someday and smile about.

Happy Birthday, beautiful. ❤️

I hope this year brings you everything your heart wishes for.

And when you read this letter years from now, I hope you remember:

This was the letter I gave you when I met you for the first time.

With love,
S.S ❤️
